# Privacy Policy — Lumi: Your Cosmos

Last updated: November 11, 2026


## 1. Who is responsible

The person responsible for data processing in connection with Lumi is **Курскиев Адам Хусенович (Adam Khusenovich Kurskiev)**, **Russian Federation** ("we").

Support and privacy contact: **kurskievadam001@gmail.com**. You may use the subject "Lumi — privacy".

This policy covers the native Lumi app for iOS. Independent services provided by Apple, email providers and advertising providers, including websites opened from the app, have their own data handling terms.

## 2. How the game works

Lumi is a general-audience puzzle game, not specifically marketed to children. You do not need an app account, name or phone number to play. The app does not request access to contacts, photos, the camera or microphone.

Core gameplay does not require our own game server. Optional advertising and external websites require internet access.

## 3. Data stored on your device

Lumi stores the board, upcoming lights, current score, moves, merges and Soft Wave charges; your personal best and discovered lights; constellations, discovery dates and selected wallpaper; sound, haptic and hint preferences; and a previous valid save. If a save cannot be read, the original unreadable data is retained separately to avoid permanent loss.

This information supports saved gameplay, rewards, discoveries and preferences. Our app code does not send your board, score, collection or backup to our server. The game does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

If system device backups are enabled, local data may be included. Apple’s services and your device settings govern those backups.

## 4. Advertising and the third-party SDK

Lumi uses **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK / Yandex Advertising Network** for ads. Its core SDK includes supporting AppMetrica components. We do not configure a separate product-analytics counter.

Rewarded ads are offered in two situations: on a full board, to clear the 18 lowest-tier lights and continue; and at zero Soft Wave charges, to receive one charge. Display begins after a separate "Watch an ad" action. Declining does not grant a reward. A new garden remains a free option on a full board, and already earned waves can be used normally.

**Preloading:** the SDK initializes and loads ads ahead of time when the app starts. Advertising network requests may therefore occur before you choose to watch an ad. Declining a particular display does not undo preloading that has already occurred.

To deliver and operate ads, the provider may receive network information such as your IP address and request parameters, device/app information, technical identifiers and advertising information. The integrated SDK’s privacy manifest declares device and advertising data categories. Actual processing depends on SDK version, configuration, device permissions and applicable requirements.

Before SDK initialization, the current app disables SDK location collection, does not assert positive user consent, and disables anonymous AppMetrica advertising reports. The app does not present an App Tracking Transparency request or request IDFA authorization. This does not mean the advertising service processes no technical data.

See [Yandex’s Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/) and the [Yandex Mobile Ads iOS privacy documentation](https://ads.yandex.com/helpcenter/en/dev/ios/app-privacy-apple) for provider information.

## 5. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your sender address, message and any attachments you voluntarily provide. We use them to answer your question, investigate an issue and handle data requests. The email provider also processes correspondence under its own terms.

Do not send account passwords, verification codes, payment details or unnecessary identity documents. They are not needed for ordinary game support.

## 6. Purposes and legal grounds

Local saves support the game features you choose to use. Support correspondence is handled to respond to your request. Advertising SDK interactions support the available advertising functionality.

The legal ground for particular processing depends on applicable law. Where consent is required, processing must rely on that consent. Where legally permitted, handling a user request, operating the service and legitimate interests may provide other grounds. This policy does not replace a separate consent or treat continued gameplay as consent to tracking or all data processing.

## 7. Recipients and international processing

Your local game progress is not provided to other players. Advertising interactions are handled by the advertising provider and its service participants as needed for their services; correspondence is handled by people and services supporting your request. Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities where legally required.

Advertising and email services may process information outside your country. Their actual providers, agreements and policies determine the locations and arrangements. This policy does not promise that all information stays in a single country. Contact us with questions about applicable safeguards.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Local saves, backup saves and preferences remain in the app’s container until the relevant data or app is deleted. Starting a new garden resets the session, keeping your personal best, collection and preferences.

To remove local Lumi data, use iOS **Delete App**. **Offload App** may retain its data. Device backups are managed separately through Apple, and restoring a backup may restore old data.

Support messages are retained while needed to resolve a request, protect lawful interests and meet applicable obligations, and should then be deleted or anonymized. The advertising provider’s own policy and applicable requirements govern its retention; we do not invent a uniform retention period for it.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete information already processed by advertising or email providers. You can contact the provider and/or us about the relevant request procedure.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You may decline individual ad displays, change game preferences, delete local app data and manage iOS backups. Lumi currently has no user account requiring deletion.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and complaints to a relevant authority. Send requests to **kurskievadam001@gmail.com**. We consider requests under applicable law and use reasonable verification measures without seeking excessive information.

We cannot remotely access your local iPhone board or collection or retrieve it based solely on an email address. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

## 10. Minors

Lumi is intended for a general audience and is not presented as a dedicated children’s service. It has no registration or birth-date entry, so it does not determine each player’s age. An App Store content rating does not replace requirements concerning minors’ information.

A parent or legal guardian who believes a child’s data has been improperly processed in connection with Lumi can contact us. We will review the request and coordinate with the advertising provider where necessary. Any future move to specifically target children requires reviewing advertising configuration and this policy before the change.

## 11. Security

The app uses iOS application storage and system protections. Support correspondence is used for support purposes, and we do not ask for account secrets. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure; we cannot guarantee that every loss or unauthorized access is impossible.

## 12. Changes

We review this policy when functionality, providers, data processing or applicable requirements change. The current version should remain available through the public link in app settings and the App Store listing. An effective date will be set on publication. Material changes will receive additional notice or consent where required.
